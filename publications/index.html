Journal articles

  1. Published1st author

    An integrated deep learning approach for enhancing brain tumor diagnosis (opens in a new tab)

    Rabeya Bashri Sumona, John Pritom Biswas, Ahmed Shafkat, Md Mahbubur Rahman, Md Omor Faruk, Yaqoob Majeed

    Healthcare Analytics (Elsevier), Vol. 8, Article 100421, 2025

    Introduces RDXNet, a hybrid of ResNet50, DenseNet121 and Xception combined through feature fusion and transfer learning, for four-class brain tumor classification on 7,023 MRI images from three public datasets. It reaches 94% accuracy, with K-fold cross-validation for robustness and Grad-CAM for interpretability.

    DOI 10.1016/j.health.2025.100421 (opens in a new tab)Project: RDXNet
