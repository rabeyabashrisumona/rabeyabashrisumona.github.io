BanglaVegNet — Bangladeshi Vegetable Image Dataset

A public image dataset of 42 vegetable classes commonly found in Bangladesh, for classification and detection research.
Status
Completed
Timeline
2024 – 2025
Area
Datasets, Computer Vision

Overview

Problem
Computer vision for agriculture and food technology needs labelled images of the produce people actually grow and buy. Many vegetables common in Bangladesh are missing or under-represented in widely used image datasets.
Approach
Collect and label photographs of 42 vegetable categories and release them openly with a persistent DOI.
My role and contribution
First author; dataset curation.
Technical stack
  • Data collection
  • Image labelling
  • Dataset curation

Contents

4,730 JPG images across 42 classes, including 1,877 raw, unprocessed images.

Classes: Arum Lobe, Ash Gourd, Beetroot, Bitter Melon, Bottle Gourd, Broccoli, Cabbage, Capsicum, Carrot, Cauliflower, Chives Onion, Chili, Coconut, Coriander, Cucumber, Eggplant, Elephant Foot Yam, Flat Bean, Garlic, Ginger, Gooseberry, Green Papaya, Green Spinach, Jicama, Kohlrabi, Lime, Malabar Spinach Seed, Okra, Onion, Plantain, Pointed Gourd, Potato, Pumpkin, Radish, Radish Leaves, Red Amaranth, Shaluk, Snake Gourd, Taro, Tomato, Yardlong Bean and Zucchini.

Intended uses

  • Image classification
  • Object detection
  • Agricultural and food-technology analysis

Access

The dataset is hosted on Mendeley Data (version 2). Download it, and check the licence and permitted uses, on the dataset record.
