3D MRI Brain Tumor Segmentation (BraTS 2020)

Status
Completed
Area

Overview

Problem
Tumor segmentation works on 3D multi-modal MRI volumes, which need different preprocessing and evaluation from 2D classification.
Approach
Prepare the BraTS 2020 volumes for training and evaluate segmentation quality with overlap metrics.
My role and contribution
Preprocessing, training pipeline and evaluation.
Technical stack
  • Python
  • Deep learning
  • 3D MRI

Work

  • Preprocessed volumetric MRI data from BraTS 2020
  • Built training pipelines for deep learning segmentation models
  • Evaluated with the Dice coefficient and Intersection over Union (IoU)

Related projects

  • RDXNet — Hybrid Brain Tumor Classification

    A hybrid model that fuses ResNet50, DenseNet121 and Xception features to classify brain MRI scans into glioma, meningioma, pituitary tumor or no tumor. Published in Healthcare Analytics (Elsevier, 2025).
