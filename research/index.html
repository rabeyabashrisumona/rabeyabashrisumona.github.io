Medical AI and Model Reliability

How reliable are deep learning models for brain tumor MRI, and how well do they hold up beyond the data they were trained on?

I work on deep learning for brain tumor classification from MRI, with attention to interpretability and to how results generalise. My published work introduced RDXNet, a hybrid of ResNet50, DenseNet121 and Xception.

Completed

  • First-author article in Healthcare Analytics (Elsevier, 2025): RDXNet reaches 94% accuracy on 7,023 MRI images across four classes, with K-fold cross-validation and Grad-CAM analysis.

In progress

  • A follow-up study on model reliability (manuscript under review, second author).

Projects

  • RDXNet — Hybrid Brain Tumor Classification
  • Brain Tumor MRI Ensemble with External Validation
  • 3D MRI Brain Tumor Segmentation (BraTS 2020)

Publications

  • Published An integrated deep learning approach for enhancing brain tumor diagnosis
  • Under review Deep Learning Ensemble for Brain Tumor Detection from MRI with External Validation

Agricultural Computer Vision

What data do vision models need to work on the produce grown and sold in Bangladesh?

BanglaVegNet is a public dataset of 4,730 images across 42 vegetable classes commonly found in Bangladesh, created to make regional produce available for classification and detection research.

Completed

  • Released BanglaVegNet on Mendeley Data with a DOI (first author).

In progress

  • Data article describing the dataset is under review at Data in Brief.
