About me

I'm a Junior AI Engineer at Dot BD Solutions and an MS student in Computer Science and Engineering at North South University. My interests span practical AI development, computer vision, deep learning, and reliable machine learning systems. Through industry projects and academic research, I explore how intelligent systems can be built, evaluated, and made more robust.

I'm interested in AI engineering opportunities, research collaborations, and conversations about reliable machine learning systems. You can reach me at rabeyabashrisumona@gmail.com.

Research

  • Graph Learning for Multi-Camera Perception. Can graph neural networks help associate the same object across cameras that never share a field of view?
