About me

I'm a Junior AI Engineer at Dot BD Solutions and a Research Assistant at Peer Research Lab, and an MS student in Computer Science and Engineering at North South University. My interests span practical AI development, computer vision, deep learning, and reliable machine learning systems. Through industry projects and academic research, I explore how intelligent systems can be built, evaluated, and made more robust.

I completed my BSc in Computer Science and Engineering at Bangladesh University of Business and Technology (BUBT) with a CGPA of 3.82/4.00, which the department formally recognised as within the top 2% of my undergraduate cohort in the CSE department.

I'm interested in AI engineering opportunities, research collaborations, and conversations about reliable machine learning systems. You can reach me at rabeyabashrisumona@gmail.com.

Research

  • Medical AI and Model Reliability. How reliable are deep learning models for brain tumor MRI, and how well do they hold up beyond the data they were trained on?
  • Graph Learning for Multi-Camera Perception. Can graph neural networks help associate the same object across cameras that never share a field of view?
  • Agricultural Computer Vision. What data do vision models need to work on the produce grown and sold in Bangladesh?
  • LLM-Assisted Software Testing. Can large language models help keep automated UI tests working when an application changes?
